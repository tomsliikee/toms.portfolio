An app of my own

Pulse

Flutter health Beta

Pulse is my own health app for Android, built with Flutter. It reads your data from Health Connect, keeps up to ten years of it on the phone and sends none of it anywhere. Its look later became Toms Expressive Design, the design language behind my other projects.
